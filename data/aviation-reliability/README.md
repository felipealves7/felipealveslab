# aviation-reliability

Saved outputs and code of the standalone flight-reliability project, used by `/lab/aviation-reliability`.
Nothing here is regenerated by this repository. Every file is a byte-for-byte copy from the project's
package `aviation-reliability-v1` (30 October 2026, SHA-256 of the ZIP
`d4ebb48a6befb176b1716fa2e199c00391e2e169b29e02fdbdb193e855f9755e`).

## Source and licence

- **Source:** US Bureau of Transportation Statistics, Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance (1987-present).
  Twenty monthly archives, January 2025 to August 2026, from https://transtats.bts.gov/PREZIP/.
  The field list https://www.transtats.bts.gov/Fields.asp?gnoyr_VQ=FGJ showed August 2026 as the latest
  month on 1 November 2026.
- **Licence:** public US federal flight records. The Data.gov record
  https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/bts-flight-data (publisher: Bureau of Transportation Statistics) gives
  https://www.usa.gov/government-works as its licence; that address redirects to USAGov's page on
  government works and copyright. No Creative Commons licence is claimed. Opened on 1 November 2026.
- **Retrieved:** 30 October 2026. A fixed snapshot; no update cadence. BTS can revise past months.
- **Synthetic:** no.
- **Personal data:** none. Rows are flights, aggregated here to months and 15-minute delay bands.

## The package in public

The whole package, code included, is public as the Kaggle dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/felipealves7/aviation-reliability-bts-foundation-v1 (Version 1, opened
signed out on 1 November 2026). `external.json` records that check and the Tableau one.

## Columns and units

`mart_month.csv`: `source_period` (YYYY-MM), `year`, `month`, then counts of flights: `scheduled`,
`cancelled`, `diverted`, `observed_arrivals` (neither cancelled nor diverted, with an arrival delay),
`on_time` (observed and less than 15 minutes late), `delayed` (observed and 15 minutes or more late),
`unclassified` (neither cancelled nor diverted, but no arrival delay), and two sums in minutes:
`arrival_delay_sum` (signed, early arrivals negative) and `positive_delay_sum` (early arrivals as zero).
Rates are sums divided by sums, as `metric-contract.json` defines.

`delay_histogram.csv`: `bin_start` in minutes (−60 holds every arrival more than 30 minutes early, 180
holds every arrival 180 minutes or more late, other bands are 15 minutes wide) and `flights`.

## Checks run on every build of this site

`scripts/aviation-page.test.mjs` and the page components stop the build when:

- a month's flights do not add up (scheduled = cancelled + diverted + observed + unclassified);
- January to August of either year differs from `cached-results.json` by a single count;
- any of the 180 monthly totals (9 measures × 20 months) differs from `independent-raw-totals.json`;
- the delay bands do not add up to the observed arrivals;
- a file in `code/` no longer matches its SHA-256 in `source-code-checksums.json`.
